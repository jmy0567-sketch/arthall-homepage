Jeongsik YI이정식

AboutWorks 14ExhibitionsCV

Jeongsik YI studied Korean ink painting at Dankook University and paints mountains and paths in ink and acrylic. He won the Grand Prize at the Chungnam Art Exhibition (2013), and his works are in collections including the Cheonan Museum of Art and Cheonan City Hall.
