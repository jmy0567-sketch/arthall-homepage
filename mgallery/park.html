박상국PARK SANG KUK

소개작품 11전시약력

한남대학교 미술교육과와 같은 대학 일반대학원 미술과를 졸업했어요. 개인전을 13회 열었고, 대한민국미술대전·단원미술대상전 심사위원을 지냈어요. 지금 연작은 「UNEXPECTEDLY MEETING A MARTIAN(뜻밖에 화성인을 만나다)」이에요.

Park Sang Kuk graduated in Art Education from Hannam University and holds an MA in Fine Arts from the same university. He has held 13 solo exhibitions and served as a juror for the Grand Art Exhibition of Korea. His current series is UNEXPECTEDLY MEETING A MARTIAN.
